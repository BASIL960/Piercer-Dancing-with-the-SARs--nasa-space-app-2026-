02 · THE PROBLEM

When storms, smoke or night close the sky, satellite cameras go blind

04 · HOW IT WORKS

From NASA data to result

1

Data in

NISAR radar over Jazan, Jun–Oct 2026: 16 change pairs and 22 brightness passes, at dusk and dawn.

→
2

Prepare

Keep the plain below 250 m. Drop striped and 24-day pairs. Wet ground = 2 dB above the dry season.

→
3

Build

Line up each pass with half-hourly NASA rain, NASA photos on 69 dates and dated official alerts.

→
4

Result

A season you can play back. Each alert pops up with that day’s rain and what the next radar pass saw.
