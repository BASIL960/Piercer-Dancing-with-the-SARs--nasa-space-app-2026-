03 · OUR SOLUTION

Live maps that replay what radar saw through the clouds

Press play on a real 2026 event and watch the ground get wet, burn or tear open, with NASA rain, photos and official alerts on one timeline.

WHAT MAKES IT NEW

07 · QUALITY

How we checked every number

1

A control for every result

Each change is compared with ground that should not have changed. Nepal: 38% of the flood corridor against 7% of the slopes beside it.

2

A range, not one number

Each threshold is nudged down and up and the result kept with its range: Jazan green-up 69% (50–77%).

3

Every number labelled

Measured, derived, reported or estimated, on screen and in one claims table with its rule and source.

4

Checked by scripts

Maps line up to 1.1 m on known points, terrain within 20 m of the research, words recomputed from data, and the whole app run in a browser.

CLAIMS WE CORRECTED
  • Nepal stripped ground: 16 km² 11.7 km²
  • Oregon radar: 72% 51%, from calibrated data
  • “Radar predicts green”: a match in place, not a forecast
STILL PROVISIONAL
  • NISAR products and IMERG rain are early releases.
  • Animation between passes is an estimate.
  • Radar shows that ground changed, not why.
