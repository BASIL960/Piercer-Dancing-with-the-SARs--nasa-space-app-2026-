    Daily timing from NASA VIIRS fire detections (375 m); burn edges from NASA HLS (Landsat + Sentinel-2, 30 m) before/after; spread within each day is interpolated outward from the previous day's edge. The burned-so-far and share tiles follow that interpolation and are estimates; the burned area itself (1,810 km²) is measured from pre- and post-fire HLS median composites (dNBR at least 0.10), inside the official NIFC perimeter. Radar: NASA-ISRO NISAR L2 GCOV, calibrated backscatter (track 114, every 12 days), read from the HDF5 files, with HV compared with the median of the three pre-fire passes (22 Jun, 4 Jul, 16 Jul); "burned hottest" = dNBR above 0.44 (7% of the burned ground). NISAR has no passes over the fire from 26 Jul to 12 Aug, so the radar pair spanning the fire is 36 days long, not 12; the land outside is measured over the same pair. Acres, containment and crew numbers are reported (NIFC, idahonews.com, Elkhorn Media Group).
    How we state numbers (the same rules on all three of our maps). Every number is measured (calibrated NASA or NISAR data), derived with a stated rule, reported (official or news) or interpolated (animation only, never quoted as a finding). Rules: radar change = 12-day NISAR coherence at least 0.20 below the pre-event baseline; radar darkening = backscatter more than 2 dB below the pre-event median; vegetation loss or gain = NDVI change of at least 0.15 across the 0.30 level; open water = MNDWI above 0.10; burned = dNBR at least 0.10. Each derived number was recomputed with its threshold nudged down and up, and is compared with a control (before the event, or ground beside it); where the result moves by more than 10%, a range is given in brackets.
