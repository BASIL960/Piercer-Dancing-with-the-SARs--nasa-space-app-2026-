    Flood route traced down the valley floor from the Copernicus 30 m elevation model, starting at the top of the debris trail seen by NASA HLS (Landsat + Sentinel-2). Surge timing uses reported times (08:37 collapse, ~09:00 Rasuwagadhi, ~10 min to Syabrubesi) and is interpolated between them. Radar: NASA-ISRO NISAR L2 GUNW coherence, track 098; the pre-flood baseline is the best of the four 12-day pairs from 8 Jun to 26 Jul. Flood corridor = within 420 m of the route; slopes = 420 m to 1.5 km. Deaths, times and the barrier lake are reported figures (Kathmandu Post, OneIndia, ICIMOD, NDRRMA). The stripped-ground counter grows with the interpolated surge; its final value is measured.
    How we state numbers (the same rules on all three of our maps). Every number is measured (calibrated NASA or NISAR data), derived with a stated rule, reported (official or news, linked) or interpolated (animation only, never quoted as a finding). Rules: radar change = 12-day NISAR coherence at least 0.20 below the pre-event baseline; vegetation loss or gain = NDVI change of at least 0.15 across the 0.30 level; open water = MNDWI above 0.10; burned = dNBR at least 0.10. Each derived number was recomputed with its threshold nudged down and up, and is compared with a control (before the event, or ground beside it); where the result moves by more than 10%, a range is given.
